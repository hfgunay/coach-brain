# KPSS (Kamu Personeli Seçme Sınavı)

Civil service selection exam in Türkiye, run by ÖSYM. Several exams share the name, so the first thing to know is
**which one** the student takes; this is personal, ask it once.

## Which KPSS

- **Lisans (university graduates)**: Genel Yetenek - Genel Kültür (GY-GK). Candidates for some posts also take Alan
  Bilgisi (field knowledge: law, economics, public administration, etc.). Teacher candidates take Eğitim
  Bilimleri and, for most branches, ÖABT (field knowledge for teachers).
- **Önlisans** and **Ortaöğretim**: only GY-GK, at their own level.
- The levels are not held every year and not in the same month. **Verify the next date** of the student's
  level in the ÖSYM calendar.

## Parts

The GY-GK test has two halves: Genel Yetenek (Türkçe, matematik) and Genel Kültür (tarih, coğrafya,
vatandaşlık, güncel bilgiler). The per-subject question counts differ by level and change over time, so look
them up in the latest ÖSYM guide before planning; don't use counts from memory.

## Format and scoring

- Multiple choice; check the number of options for the student's level in the guide.
- Four wrong answers cancel one correct answer.

## What to verify with web search

`site:osym.gov.tr KPSS <year> kılavuz`: the date, which tests the student's level takes, the question counts per
subject, and for ÖABT the field's topics.
