# TUS (Tıpta Uzmanlık Eğitimi Giriş Sınavı)

Entrance exam for medical specialty training in Türkiye, run by ÖSYM. Taken by medical school graduates and
final-year students.

## When

- Twice a year: the 1st period (1. dönem) in spring, the 2nd period (2. dönem) in late summer.
- One day, two sessions: the basic sciences test in the morning, the clinical sciences test in the afternoon.

## Format and scoring

- Multiple choice, five options (A-E), one correct answer.
- Four wrong answers cancel one correct answer; a blank costs nothing. So guessing blindly doesn't pay, an
  educated guess between two options does.
- Scores are computed separately with different weights for basic and clinical (K and T scores); which score
  counts depends on the specialty the student wants. Ask which specialty they aim for only when it changes the
  plan.
- Questions are mostly case vignettes in clinical sciences and mechanism/recall questions in basic sciences.
  Write practice questions in Turkish, in that style, unless the student asks otherwise.

## What to look up when the profile isn't enough

Search the official site first (`site:osym.gov.tr TUS <year>`): the exam date and application dates of the next
period, any change in test structure or scoring. Prep-course analyses of the last papers can show which topics
came up most, but they are secondary sources; say so when you use them.
